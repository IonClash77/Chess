YOUR MAP IS ALIVE. YOUR DICE ARE TIRING.

In classic dungeon crawlers, rooms sit quietly waiting for you to open the door. In Dead Sector, every decision echoes two rooms ahead.

🫁
Pillar 01 // The Core Engine

THE BREATHING STAMINA POOL

Your D6 pool is both your fury and your shield. Every round, split your dice between violent Attacks and unrolled Guards. Swing with everything to drop a Screamer before it shrieks—or play it safe so a Sprinter doesn't tear your throat out. Roll a 1, and that die locks up from exhaustion.

"Do I spend my last Grit to reroll that 1, or save it for the Riot Zombie down the hall?"
📢
Pillar 02 // Dynamic Map Decay

DECIBELS & DARKNESS

Fire a shotgun or pry open a security gate with a crowbar, and your Decibel Tracker climbs. You add your current Noise level directly to your room-generation rolls—meaning the loud gunfight you survived in the Lobby literally draws swarms, blown-in windows, and fire into the rooms ahead.

Power out? Roll 2D6: the lower die is how far your flashlight beam reaches into the dark.
🪟
Pillar 03 // Modern Architecture

GLASS, DRYWALL & BIOMASS

Peek through Plate-Glass Storefronts to map the loot and undead inside before entering—just remember that fighting in the hallway will shatter the glass and pull them out. Cornered by spreading biomass? Spend a Sledgehammer point to draw your own exit straight through the drywall.

Lock doors and draw barricades behind you so your extraction route doesn't become a tomb.
4 STANDALONE SCENARIO TAPES

CHOOSE YOUR SLAUGHTERHOUSE

Each setting uses the same 2D6 split-dice engine to transform your graph paper into a completely different survival nightmare.

TAPE 01 // VERTICAL BREACH Difficulty: ★★☆

THE DEAD MEGAMALL & HIGH-RISE

Ascend a 40-story quarantine tower or cross a sprawling dead mall to reach rooftop evac. Features plate-glass storefronts, security shutters, pharmacy loot runs, and atrium escalators.

Signature Rule: Split-Dice Blueprints (Die 1 draws room footprint; Die 2 spawns storefront purpose & loot).
TAPE 02 // THE GRIDLOCK Difficulty: ★★★

HIGHWAY OVERPASS MILE 14

A six-lane highway overpass choked with jackknifed 18-wheelers, FEMA tents, and military blockades with a sheer drop on both sides. Squeeze between bumpers or breach the back of an ambulance for meds.

Signature Rule: Shifting Walls — rolling doubles on an encounter pushes vehicle clusters down the page under the weight of the horde.
TAPE 03 // TOTAL BLACKOUT Difficulty: ★★★

SUB-BASEMENT GENERATOR RUN

The grid has failed. Navigate pitch-black maintenance tunnels where your flashlight beam only reveals the front half of any room you open. Step into the unmapped dark to find the exits.

Signature Rule: Flashlight Cones & Noise Amplification — gunshots echo in concrete tunnels (+2 Decibels per shot).
TAPE 04 // INFECTION VECTOR Difficulty: ★★★★

CDC BIO-LAB CONTAINMENT

Grab the antiviral samples on Sub-Level 3 and make it back to the airlock alive. Rolling doubles shades the room in living biomass that creeps through open doorways into cleared rooms every turn milestone.

Signature Rule: The Tethered Escape — weld doors shut and draw barricades on your way in, or your return trip becomes a death trap.

SURVIVOR LEVELING & WORKBENCH MODS

  • Stamina Pool Growth: Start at Level 1 with 3D6. Add +1D6 at Levels 3, 6, and 9 (Max 6D6).
  • Grit (Re-Rolls): Start with 1 Grit per fight to re-roll a single Attack die (essential for rescuing a rolled 1 before it locks!). Scales up to 4 Grit at higher levels.
  • Second Wind (Recovery): Once per combat, spend your turn rolling 0 Attack dice to unlock 1–2 Exhausted dice (1s) back into your active pool.
  • Settlement Workbench Mods: Attach Weighted Head (+1 damage when committing 3+ Attack dice) or Ergonomic Grip (ignore the first 1 rolled each fight).
